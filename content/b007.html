Meeqaat se guzarte waqt sirf usi par Ihraam wajib hai jiska Hajj ya Umra ka irada ho, aur jiska irada na ho to us par Ihraam lazim nahi, lihaza jo log sirf Taif , Ji‘ranah aur Madina ki ziyarat ko jaate hain aur waapsi par Umrah ka irada nahi rakhte, un par Ihraam zaroori nahi, Albatta sunnat hai. (2) Agar asal iraada duniyavi kaam (tijarat, taleem, mulaqat waghera) ka ho:To Miqat se Ihram baandhna zaroori nahi. Jab baad mein Hajj ya Umrah ka pakka iraada bane, to wahin se Ihram baandh le (maslan: Jeddah se). (Al-Iydaah: 121, Ujalah al-Muhtaaj: 2/584)

Jeddah mein muqeem afraad ki meeqaat

Jeddah me muqeem afraad ki meeqaat khud Jiddah hi hai; lihāza jab un ka Hajj ya Umrah ka irādah ho to un par lāzim hai ke Jeddah hi se ahraam bāndhe۔

Tawaf ke dauran chappal apne saath rakhna

Agar Haram me chappal gum hone ka andesha ho to tawaf ke dauran chappal ko kisi saaf thele ya bag me daal kar apne saath rakhna jaiz hai. Albata is baat ka ahtimam zaroori hai ke chappal par koi najasat na ho, agar lagi ho to pehle use paak kar liya jaye, phir bag me rakha jaye. (Mugni l-Muhtaj: 2/262)

Mayyat ki taraf se Hajj o Umrah karna

Agar kisi mayyat ka ek baar bhi Hajj ya Umrah nah hua ho to uski wasiyat ya ijaazat ke baghair bhi uski taraf se ek martaba Hajj ya Umrah kar sakte hain. Agarche zindagi mein mayyit par hajj ya umrah farz nahin hua tha. Agar ek martaba kisi ki taraf se Hajj ya Umrah ho chuka ho to doosri martaba uski taraf se karne ke liye uski wasiyat ya ijaazat zaroori hai. Agar uski wasiyat nahi hai to Umrah apni taraf se karein aur sawaab mein us mayyat ko bhi shamil karei. (Al-Majmoo‘: 7/81, Al-Bayan: 4/52)

Hawai jahaz ke dar ki wajah se Hajj ya Umrah ke liye naaib banana.

Hawai jahaz mein baithne ke khauf ki wajah se kisi dusre ko apni taraf se Hajj ya Umrah ka naaib banana durust nahi, naaib usi waqt banaya ja sakta hai jab aadmi ko aisi bimaari ho jis se accha hone ki umeed na ho ya aisa boodha ho jo sawari par baithne ki qudrat hi na rakhta ho; magar bohot hi mushakkat ke baad; lihaza sirf dar ki buniyad par doosra shakhs bhejne se Hajj o Umrah ada nahi honge. (Al-Iydaah, pg: 117)

Aurat ke liye Hajj o Umrah ki Farziyat aur Bagair Mehram Safar ka Hukm

Aurat par Hajj wa Umrah farz hone ke liye zaroori hai ke uske saath shauhar ya mahram ya bharosa mand auratein ho, albatta farz hone ke baad is fariza ki adaaigi ke liye tanha bhi safar kar sakti hai. Sunnat Hajj wa Umrah ke safar mein shauhar ya mahram ka saath hona zaroori hai, bharosa mand auraton ya sirf tours ke saath nahi jaa sakti. (Hashiyatul Jamal: 2/384)

Safar se pehle aurat ko haiz "MC" aaye

Agar kisi aurat ke apne ghar (watan) se Umrah ke liye Makkah rawaana hone se pehle na-paaki (Haiz "MC") ke din shuru ho jaayein to ab iski do sooratein hain.

Pehli soorat: Yeh aurat na-paaki ki haalat mein hi apne watan se Ihraam ka gusl karke Umrah ki Niyyat karegi aur Makkah ke liye rawana hogi. Aur paaki aane tak Ihraam ki pabandiyon mein ruki rahegi. Phir paaki ke baad gusl karke Umrah ke Arkaan poora karegi. Agar aurat na-paaki ke dino mein paaki ka itna zamana paati hai ke gusl karke tawaaf kar sake, to iski bhi ijaazat hai. Agar iska tawaaf paaki ki haalat mein waqia hota hai to iske baad sa’ee aur baalo ko kaat kar Ihraam se nikal sakti hai aur dam bhi dene ki zaroorat nahi.

Doosri soorat: Agar aurat mashaqqat ke khauf se Ihraam ki Niyyat kiye bagair Makkah chali jaaye to aisi soorat mein use aik dam dena zaroori hoga. Phir paaki aane ke baad woh apne room hi se Ihraam ki namaz aur Umrah ki Niyyat karke Umrah ke Arkaan adaa karegi. Masjid -e- Ayesha wagairah jaane ki zaroorat nahi. Isliye ke ye aurat watan ki meeqaat Ya’lamlam se Ihraam nah bandhne ki wajah se dam adaa kar rahi hai. Dam ke bagair sirf Masjid-e-Ayesha wagairah se Umrah karna kaafi nahi. (Mugnil Muhtaaj: 2/228)

Aurat agar haiz me ho aur watan wapsi ka waqt aa jaye to kya kare?

Agar aurat ko Makkah mein qiyam ke dauran haiz aa jaye aur woh umrah ka ihram baandh chuki ho aur watan wapsi ka waqt qareeb ho, to us ke liye teen sooratein hain:

1️⃣ Wapsi mein ta’kheer karne ki koshish kare.

2️⃣ Goli ya kisi jaiz tadbeer ke zariye kam az kam ek din ki paaki haasil kar ke umrah ada kare.

3️⃣ Agar khoon band na ho, to safai ka ghusl karke poori ehtiyat ke saath isi haalat mein tawaaf aur umrah ke baqi a’maal mukammal kar le.

Lekin is soorat mein ek oont ka dam wajib hoga، aur agar is ki taqat na ho to ek bakra ya mendha ka dam lazim hoga۔

(Tohfatul Mohtaaj 4/84, Fiqhi Seminar 2019)

Safar Shuru Karne Se Pehle Shohar Ka Intiqaal

Agar kisi aurat ka Hajj wa umrah ka safar shuru karne se pehle shohar ka intiqaal ho jaaye jabke uska ticket ban chuka ho, to is surat mein uske liye safar karna jaaiz nahi hai. Uska apne maqam par char mah aur das din ki iddat poori karna zaroori hai. Haa agar shohar ke intiqaal ki khabar safar shuru hone ke baad mili ho aur usne yeh safar shohar ki ijaazat se hi kiya ho, to ab use safar ke jaari rakhne ya waapas aane ke darmiyaan ikhtiyaar hai. (Kanz ur-Ragibeen: 482)

Zamzam Ka Pani Peene Ka Tareeqa

Zamzam ka paani bhi baith kar peena sunnat hai. (Fath ul-Bari: 5616) Qibla rukh hokar piye, aur Bismillah parh kar piye, aur yeh dua bhi parhe:

اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّی أَسْئَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَّافِعًا وَّرِزْقًا وَّاسِعًا وَّ شِفَاءً مِّنْ کُلِّ دَاءٍ.

Phir magfirat aur bimaari se shifa ya kisi aur garz ki niyyat kare, aur ise apne chehre, sar aur jism ko mal le, aur apne watan waapsi ke waqt ise apne saath bhi le jaaye, taake doosre log bhi iski barkat se faida uthaaye. (Tuhfat ul-Muhtaj: 4/144)

Gair Muslim Ko Zamzam Aur Khajoor Dena

Gair-Muslimon ko Zamzam aur khajoor dena jaaiz hai aur saath yeh dua karein ke Allah Ta’ala in mubaarak cheezon ki barkat se is gair-Muslim bhai ka seenah imaan ke liye khol de. Hadees-e-sahih se saabit hai ke Zamzam mein shifa hai (Ibne Majah: 3062) aur kufr se barh kar koyi ruhaani bimaari nahi ho sakti. (Kitab ul-Fatawa li Khalid Saifullah: 4/84)

Imaam-e-Haram Ki Iqtida Mein Raaste Mein Namaz

Agar Imaam masjid mein ya usse baaahar ho aur muqtadi raaste mein ya building ke neeche ho, to us Imaam ki iqtida sahi hone ke liye do shartein hain:

Pehli shart: Masjid ke aakhri kinaare ya masjid ke baahar raaste mein bani gaye har do safo ke darmiyaan ka faasla 300 zira (450 fit / 145 meter) se ziyada nah ho. Yani Haram se baahar jo aakhri saf bani hai, usse le kar Haram ke raaste mein jo doosri saf bani hai, in dono ke darmiyaan ka faasla 450 fit se ziyada nah ho. Phir doosri aur teesri saf mein bhi faasla isi tarah 450 fit se ziyada nah ho to uske darmiyaan kahi bhi namaz padhe to iqtida durust hogi.

Doosri shart: Imaam aur muqtadi ke darmiyaan aisa haa’il nah ho jo guzarne aur dekhne se roke. Agar koyi building hi ke kisi manzila par namaz shuru kare to yeh kaafi nahi, balke iske liye zaroori hai ke aisi jagah ikhtiyar kare jaha se agar Imaam tak chal kar jaana chahe to ba-asaani jaa sake. Building ke nichle hisse (Ground Floor) mein haraj nahi, albatta (Ground Floor) chaaro taraf se mukammal band ho to namaz ke waqt masjid ki taraf ka hissa ek aadmi ke guzarne ke baqadr khula rakhna zaroori hai ya aisa autometic darwaza ho jo qareeb aate hi khul jaaye, warna iqtida durust nahi hogi. (Tuhfat ul-Muhtaj: 2/315)

Makka Mukarrama ke Bin Dawood Tower me namaz

Makkah Mukarramah ke Bin Dawood Tower ki taqreeban chauthain manzil par waqea us masjid mein, jo ek seedhi ke zariye barah-e-rast Haram Shareef ke sahan se muttasil hai, wahan se Imam-e-Haram ki iqtedaa mein namaz ada karna Sahih hai.(Minhaaj-ut-Talibeen: p. 133)

Mataaf mein Imaam se aage kharay hona

Mataaf mein Imam se aage kharay ho kar namaz pardhna theek nahi hai. Is liye koshish yeh honi chahiye ke namaz aisi jagah par padhi jaye jo Imam ke muqaam se peechay ho — yaani Kaaba ke baaqi teen tarafon mein. Lekin agar majboori ho, to Imam Shafi‘i ke qoul-e-qadeem aur Imam Maalik ke nazdeek aisi surat mein namaz Durust hoti hai (Al-Majmoo‘: 4/300, Al-Mudawwanatul-Kubra: 1/175)

Haram-e-Makkah mein ek namaz ka sawab ek lakh ke baraabar hai to haram se kya murad hai?

Is silsile mein do qoul hain:

(1) Haram se murad Masjid-e-Haram hai.

(2) Haram se mukammal Haram murad hai, isi doosre qoul ko Imaam Nawawi Rahimahullah ne sahih qaraar diya hai. (Fath-ul - Bari: 1190)

Peshaab ki takleef wala shakhs Ihraam mein kya kare?

Jis shakhs ko Peshaab ke Qatre tapakne ki shikayat ho wo shakhs haalat-e-Ihraam mein mukammal ehtiyaat ki koshish karega, taake Peshaab ke Qatre Ihraam ki chaadar ko nah lag jaayein warna wo chaadar napaak ho jaayegi, jabke tawaaf ke liye chaadar ka paak hona zaroori hai.

Lihaaza, yeh shakhs apni Peshaab ki jagah tissue paper rakh kar use rubber ke zariye baandh sakta hai, is se fidya laazim nahi hoga. Albatta agar is ke saath wo chaddi (underwear ya pampers) bhi pehne to ab kam az kam 7 kilo 200 gram anaaj bator fidya dena laazim hoga.

Agar is shakhs ke Qatre musalsal jaari ho to wo tawaaf se pehle achchi tarah safaayi haasil kare, tissue paper ko badal de aur wuzu kare, aur wuzu mein Niyyat is tarah kare: “Farz wuzu karta hu namaz ke jaaiz hone ke liye aur napaaki door hone ke liye.” Aur phir bila takheer jaldi se umrah ka farz tawaaf adaa kare.

(Tanbeeh) Peshaab ke Qatre ya hawa ke baar baar nikalne wala bimaar shakhs apni tahaarat se sirf ek hi farz adaa kar sakta hai, chahe wo farz tawaaf ho ya farz namaz, albatta sunnat tawaaf aur sunnat namaz jitni chahe parh sakta hai. Dauran-e-tawaaf aur namaz jo Qatre nikalenge wo maaf rahenge.n (Hashiyatul Jamal: 1/162, Mughni al-Muhtaaj: 1/84)

Mustahaza aurat ka hukm:

Jis aurat ka ayaam-e-haiz ke alawa mukammal mahina khoon nikalta ho, uska hukm bhi upar zikr kardah Peshaab ki takleef wale shakhs ki tarah hai; lihaza yeh aurat bhi tawaf se pehle achhi tarah safai hasil karegi aur wuzu karegi aur wuzu mein niyyat is tarah karegi: "Farz wuzu karti hoon namaz ke jaaiz hone ke liye aur napaaki door hone ke liye". Aur phir bila takheer jaldi se apne umrah ka farz tawaf ada karegi. Isi tarah woh apni taharat se sirf ek farz (tawaf ya namaz) mein se koi ek ada kar sakti hai, aur sunnat namaz aur tawaf jitne chahe kar sakti hai. Aur jo aurat mustahaza nahi hai, uske tawaf ke liye mukammal paaki ka hona shart hai. (Hashiyat-ul-Jamal: 1/162, Mughni al-Muhtaaj: 1/84)

Jiddah Airport par kisi ka Ihraam utaar diya jaaye ya Makkah Mukarramah pohanchne ke baad bimaari ki waja se umrah nah kar sake

Iski do sooratein hain:

Pehli soorat: Jiddah Airport par kisi ke Ihraam ke kapde qanooni karwaayi ke sabab utaar diye jaayein aur use saade kapre pehnne par majboor karke Makkah rawaana kiya jaaye, aur wo umrah ki Niyyat kar chuka ho to uska umrah ka Ihraam nahi utrega. uske zimme mamnooa libaas pehnne ke sabab fidya hoga, albatta uska Ihraam umrah ke aamaal poora karne ke baad hi uthrega.

Isi tarah koyi shakhs Makkah Mukarramah pohanchne par bimaar par jaaye aur umrah ke aamaal nah kar sake, to jab bhi use bimaari se shifa haasil hogi aur uske baad wo aamaal-e-umrah ko anjaam dega, to uska Ihraam khul jaayega. Magar ye ke Niyyat ke waqt shart lagayi ho ke agar umrah ke poora karne se koyi rukaawat pesh aaye, to mai Ihraam khol dunga, to ab aamaal-e-umrah mukammal karne se pehle bhi Ihraam khol sakta hai.

Doosri soorat: Kisi ko apne watan wapas kar diya jaaye, to ye *muḥṣar* ke hukm mein hoga. Lihaza, agar usne Ihraam ki shart mein zabah ka tazkira kiya ho to wahi par ek dam dena hoga, phir baal saaf karye ya chhote kare, dono ko Ihraam se halaal hone ki Niyyat se kare.

Bimaari wagairah ki wajah se bhi agar Ihraam khol kar ghar lautne ki zarurat pesh aaye, to isi tarah Ihraam se halaal ho sakte hain.

Albatta, agar usne Ihraam ki shart mein zabah ka tazkira nah kiya ho ya Ihraam bandhte waqt ye Niyyat ki ho ke koyi rukaawat pesh aaye to mai Ihraam se nikal jaunga, to is soorat mein dam waajib nahi hoga. Sirf Niyyat aur baal saaf karne ya chhote karne se halaal ho jaayega.

Aur agar kisi ko dushman ki taraf se Hajj ya umrah karne se roka jaaye, to har-haal mein dam dena waajib hai. (Mugni al-Muhtaaj 2/343)

Hawaayi jahaaz ke khane ka kya hukm hai?

Hawaayi jahaaz ka khana agar chicken aur gosht ke qabeel se hai to is baat ki tehqiq zaroori hai ke wo musalman ka zabah kiya hua hai ya nahi. Is ke liye agar koyi mo’tabar khabar dene wala shakhs maujood nah ho to andaze se faisla kiya jaaye.

Andaza ye hai ke agar kisi musalman mulk se jahaaz ki uraan ho to gaalib gumaan ye hai ke wo gosht halaal hai, aur agar gair musalman mulk se jahaaz chala ho to gaalib gumaan iske haraam hone ka hai. Hamare mulk Bharat (India) mein ziyada tar gair musalman abaad hain, is liye yaha ke gosht se ijtinaab zaroori hai.

Is liye jahaaz aur train mein veg khane ka ehtimaam karein aur non-veg chicken, mutton wagairah se ijtinaab karein. (Kitaabul-Fatawa: 6/188, Al-Mugni: 6/134)

Waqt se pehle Juma ki sunnat parhne ka hukm

Juma ke roz Haramain Shareefain mein Hambali maslak ke Aitibaar se Juma ke pehli azaan waqt se pehle hi di jaati hai, jabke is waqt Juma ka waqt nahi hota. Juma ka waqt zohar ki tarah zawal se hi shuru hota hai.

Is liye waqt se pehle hone waali azaan ke baad Juma ki Sunnat parhna sahih nahi hai, zawaal ke baad khutba-e-Juma jald shuru hone ke sabab sunnat ke liye waqt nahi milta hai, is liye Juma ke pehle ki sunnat bhi Juma ke baad hi padhe. (Majmoo: 4/511)

Hajj o Umrah mein selfie aur video ki mazammat

Hajj o Umrah khalis ibadat hain, jin ka maqsad Allah ki raza, aajizi aur ikhlaas hai. Afsos ke aaj kal baaz log isey video, selfie aur tasveeron ke zariye dikhawey ka zariya bana lete hain. Ye amal:

Lihaza haji o mutamir ko chahiye ke woh in qeemti lamhaat ko sirf dua, zikr aur ikhlaas se sajayen, na ke camera aur tasveeron se. Yahi haqiqi kamiyabi aur Hajj o Umrah ki asal rooh hai. (Sharh Muslim lin-Nawawi: 2104)

Al-Widaayi Tawaaf

Jab bhi kisi ka Makkah se 81 kilometer ka faasla tay karne ka iraada ho, masalan apne watan mein waapsi ya Madinah wagairah jaana chahta ho, to aise shakhs par Al-Widaayi Tawaaf karna waajib hai.

Al-Widaayi Tawaaf mein nah kaandha khula rakhe aur nah hi pehle teen chakkaro mein tez chale. Al-Widaayi Tawaaf apne saare mashaagil se faarig hone ke baad ain nikalte waqt karna chahiye. Iske baad Makkah se fauran nikalna zaroori hai. Ab khareed-o-farokht mein masgool hona durust nahi hai, warna dobara tawaaf karna padega. Albatta safar ke liye khaane peene wagairah ka tosha khareed sakte hain. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 443)

Al-Widaayi Tawaaf ki niyyat

“Main Allah Ta’ala ke liye waajib al-widaayi tawaaf ki niyyat karta hu.”

Alwidaa Tawaf ke baad Makkah na chhodna aur Haram mein namaz ke liye aana ka hukm

Al-Widaayi Tawaaf karne ke baad agar bagair uzr ziyada dair thehre, ya thehre nahi lekin kisi aise kaam mein masgool ho jaaye jiska ta’alluq safar se nah ho, jaise samaan khareed ne mein, dosto ki mulaqaat mein ya kisi bimaar ki iyaadat mein, to iska al-widaayi tawaaf shumaar nahi hoga, balke dobara al-widaayi tawaaf karna zaroori hoga.

Aur agar al-widaayi tawaaf karne ke baad aise kaam mein masgool ho jaaye jiska ta’alluq safar se ho, jaise apna samaan jama karne mein lag jaaye, ya buson ke intizaam aur gaadiyon ki traffic ki wajah se Makkah se jald nikalne mein dushwari pesh aaye, to chand ghante zarurat ki wajah se takheer karne se al-widaayi tawaaf par koyi asar nahi padega, na Iaadah waajib hoga nah dam. Phir wo al-widaayi tawaaf ke baad bhi Masjid-e-Haraam mein aa kar farz wa sunnat namaz parhna chahe, ya mazeed sunnat tawaaf karna chahe, to jaaiz rahega. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 445)

Haaizah aurat par alvida’yi tawaaf

Haaiz aur nifaas waali aurat par alvida’yi tawaaf waajib nahi hai aur nah hi us par dam dena zaroori hai. Lekin use Masjid-e-Haraam ke darwaaze ke baahar khara ho kar waapsi ki dua karna mustahab hai. Al-Wida'yi Tawaaf ke baad ki dua aindah duaaon ke bayan mein aa rahi hai. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 444)

Alvida’yi tawaaf ke baad ki namaz

Alvida’yi tawaaf ke baad Maqam-e-Ibrahim ke peeche sunnat alvida’yi tawaaf ki do rakat adaa karen, aur agar mumkin ho to Multazam (jo Hajr-e-Aswad aur Kaaba ke darwaze ke darmiyaan hai) se chipak kar khoob dua karen. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 447)

Alvida’yi tawaaf ke bagair Makkah se nikalna

Jis par alvida’yi tawaaf waajib ho aur woh ise bagair uzr ke chhor de, to woh gunahgar hoga aur us par dam laazim hoga. agarche use masle ka ilm na ho, ya ilm ho magar woh Tawaaf-e-Wadaa karna bhool jaye, tab bhi us par dam zaroori hoga." Agar yeh shakhs 81 km ke andar hai to tawaaf ke liye lautna waajib hai, warna lautna zaroori nahi hai, sirf dam dena kaafi hai. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 444)

Umrah kar ke fauran Madina ya watan lautne waale par al-wida’yi tawaaf

Agar koyi Madina se taxi wagairah karke Makkah aaye aur sirf umrah kar ke fauran Madina ya watan wa ghairah waapas jaane ka iraadah ho, to iske liye bhi umrah ke aamaal se mukammal faarig hone ke baad alag se alvida’yi tawaaf karna zaroori hai. (Hashiyatul Jamal: 4/175)

Taif ke safar par Alwida'i Tawaf ka hukum

Makkah Mukarrama se Taif jaane ke mukhtalif raste hain. Agar koi shakhs aise raste se Taif jaaye jiski masafat (taqriban 81 kilometer) ke barabar ya us se zyada ho, to us par Alwida'i Tawaf wajib hoga. Albata agar woh kisi qareebi raste se Taif jaaye jahan itni masafat poori na ho, to us par Alwida'i Tawaf lazim nahi.(Al-Majmoo: 8/187)

Masjid-e-Ayesha se umrah ka hukm

Chaaro Imaam yaani Imaam Shafi’I, Imaam Abu Hanifa, Imaam Malik, aur Imaam Ahmad رحمہم اللہ ke nazdeek Masjid-e-Ayesha (Taneem) se umrah karna jaaiz hai. (Al-Fiqhul-Islami wa Adillatuhu: 3/130). Hazrat Jaabir ﷜ se marwi hai ke Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ne Hazrat Abdur Rahman bin Abi Bakr ﷜ ko hukm diya ke woh Hazrat Ayesha ﷞ ke saath Taneem jaayein aur waha se unko umrah ke Ihraam ki niyyat karaa ke lekar aayein. (Bukhari: 1785, Muslim: 1211)

Baar-baar Hajj aur Umrah karna behtar hai ya doosron ko is ke liye rawaana karna ya zarurat mandon ki madad karna?

Hajj aur Umrah dono mustaqil ibaadatein hain jinki adaaigi par Allah Ta’ala ki taraf se ajr-e-azeem ka wadaa hai, jaise ke is silsile mein kayi ahaadees marwi hain:

(1)Hazrat Abu Huraira ﷜ se marwi hai ke aap ﷺ ne farmaya: Ek Umrah doosre Umrah tak darmiyaani gunaho ka kaffara hai, aur maqbool Hajj ka badla jannat hai. (Bukhari: 1773, Muslim: 1349)

(2)Hazrat Abu Huraira ﷜ se marwi hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ se daryaaft kiya gaya ke konsa amal sab se afzal hai? Aap ﷺ ne farmaya “Allah aur uske Rasool par imaan lana.” Kaha gaya, iske baad konsa? Aap ﷺ ne farmaya “Allah ki raah mein jihaad karna.” Kaha gaya, phir kya hai? Aap ﷺ ne farmaya “Hajj-e-Maqbool.” (Bukhari: 26, Muslim: 1349)

(3)Hazrat Abu Saeed Khudri ﷜ se marwi hai ke aap ﷺ ne farmaya: Allah Ta’ala ka irshad hai: “Beshak bande ko maine tandurusti di, aur uske karobaar mein kushaadgi paida ki, phir paanch saal (aur ek riwayat mein chaar saal) is haalat mein guzarte hain ke wo mere paas safar karke nahi aata to wo (bari khair se) mahroom hai.” (Sahih Ibne Hibban: 3705, Walohu mutabi’at ‘ind Al-Tabrani fil-Awsat: (486) fihi zikru arba’atu A'wam)

Isi tarah baaz salafe saaliheen se bhi kasrat se Hajj wa Umrah karna manqool hai, jaise Muhammad bin Zanboor Makki (RH) ne assi(80) Hajj kiye (Tahzheebul-Kamal: 5220), Muhriz bin Salama Adni(RH) ne tiraasi(83) Hajj kiye. (Tahzheebul-Kamal: 5802), Amr bin Maimoon (RH) ne 60 Hajj aur 60 Umrah kiye. (Hilyat ul-Awliya: 4:148)

Is liye doosro ko Is ke liye rawaana karne ya unki sadqa wagairah ke zariye madad karne ke bajaaye, bazaate khud hi aadmi is ibaadat ko anjaam de ye zyadaa behtar hai, aur doosre logon ke haq mein duayein kare. Albatta, agar iske walidain ne ab tak is farz ko anjaam nahi diya ho aur walidain ka aajiz hone ya intiqaal kar jaane ki wajah se, to ye shakhs apne taraf se ek martaba Hajj wa Umrah karne ke baad, bator ehsan wa naiki walidain ki taraf se bhi kar sakta hai. (Fatawa al-Lajnatud-Daimah: 11/66 (6909)

Kaaba se watan rawangi ka tareeqa

Kaaba se watan rawangi ke waqt uske nazron se ojhal hone tak uske firaq wa judaaigi par afsos karte huwe baar baar palat kar deedaar karte rahe. Haram se nikalte waqt Kaaba ki taraf peeth karke nikalna chahiye. Kaaba ki taraf rukh karke ulte qadam lootna makrooh hai. (Al-Majmoo’ 8/271)

Namazi ke samne se guzarne ka hukm

Kaaba’tullah (Masjide Haraam), Masjide Nabawi aur doosri tamaam masaajid wagairah mein namazi ke samne se guzarne ka hukm ye hai ke agar namazi ke samne deewar ya sutoon wagairah ka sutra maujood ho, to uske aur sutra ke darmiyaan se guzarna haraam hai. Agar namazi ke samne sutra nah ho ya sutra namazi ke qadam se teen zira (yaani saare chaar fit) se zyadaa faasle par ho, to guzarna haraam nahi hai, lekin afzal ye hai ke nah guzarain. (Mugni al-Muhtaj 1/420)

Watan wapas hone ka amal

(1)Basti mein pohanchne se thore pehle ye dua padhe:

لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ، وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ، وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، آئِبُوْنَ ، تَآئِبُوْنَ، عَابِدُوْنَ، سَاجِدُوْنَ لِرَبِّنَا، حَامِدُوْنَ، صَدَقَ اللّٰهُ وَعْدَهٗ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهٗ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهٗ (Bukhari 1797)

(2)Basti mein daakhil hone ke baad pehle masjid jaakar do rakat adaa karna sunnat hai. (Bukhari 3088)

(3)Jab ghar pohanche, to ghar mein bhi do rakat parh kar dua kare aur Allah ka shukar adaa kare. (Al-Izaah 563)

(4)Safar se waapsi ke waqt apne ghar walo ke liye kuch hadya lekar aaye. (Qalyoobi 2/190)

(5)Hajj wa Umrah se lautne waale ke liye ye dua de:

تَقَبَّلَ اللهُ حَجَّكَ (agar Umrah hai to ‘حَجَّكَ’ ki jagah ‘عُمْرَتَكَ’ kahe) وَغَفَرَ ذَنْبَكَ وَاَخْلَفَ عَلَیْكَ نَفَقَتَكَ

Tarjuma: Allah tere Hajj/Umrah ko qabool farmaye, aur tere gunahon ko bakhshe, aur tere kharch ko tujh par waapas farmaye. (Qalyoobi 2/190)

(6)Haaji ko chahiye ke wo bhi mulaqat karne walon ke liye magfirat ki dua kare aur yun kahe: *يَغْفِرُ اللهُ لَنَا وَلَكُمْ *. Agarche wo dua ka mutalaba nah karein, fuqahe kiraam ne zikr kiya hai keh Haaji ke lautne ke din se chalis(40) roz tak us se magfirat ki dua ka sawal kiya jaaye. (Qalyoobi 2/190)

Hazrat Abu Huraira ﷜ se marwi hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: Hajj aur Umrah karne waale Allah ke wafd hain. Agar ye Allah se dua maangein to Allah use qubool karta hai, aur agar wo is se magfirat chahte hain to unhein maaf kar deta hai. (Ibne Majah 2892)

Safar se waapsi par khaane ki dawat karna

Hajj, Umrah ya doosre safar se ghar lautne ke baad dawat karna sunnat hai. Hazrat Jaabir ﷜ kehte hain ke Rasoolullah ﷺ jab (Gazwa-e-Tabook se) Madina tashreef laye to oonnt ya gaay zabah ki. (Bukhari: 3089)

Hamare yahan Hajj ya umrah se pehle dawat karte hain jab ke sunnat tareeqa yeh hai ke waapsi ke baad dawat karein. Is dawat ka naam “Naqee’ah” hai, yani lambe safar se waapsi par dawat karna. Yeh dawat musaafir kare ya doosra koyi shakhs musaafir ki aamad par dawat ka intizaam kare. (Al-Haawi Al-Kabeer: 9/555)

Safar-e-Hajj o Umra me Khareedari (Shopping)

Hajj o Umra aik azeem ibadat aur roohani safar hai, jiska maqsad Allah Ta‘ala ki Raza, gunahon ki maafi aur nekion ka husool hai. Yeh waqt bazaron aur khareedari me zaya karne ke liye nahi, dunyawi khareedari to apne watan mein bhi mumkin hai. Lekin Baitullah ke saaye mein guzare gaye lamhat aur Madina Munawwara ki haazri ka dobara naseeb hona yaqini nahi.

Is liye har Haj or Umrah karne wale ko chahiye ke zyada se zyada waqt ‘Umrah ,Tawaf, Namaz, Tilawat aur Zikr-e-ilahi mein lagaye taake yeh safar aakhirat ke liye behtareen sarmaya ban sake. Jaisa ke Hadees Mubarak me hai:Rasool Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Baitullah ka Tawaf, Safa wa Marwah ki Sa‘i aur Jamarat ki Rami isi liye rakhi gayi hai taake Allah Ta‘ala ka Zikr qaim kiya jaye.” (Abu Dawood: 1888)Pas maaloom hua ke is safar ka asal maqsad dunyawi masroofiyat nahi, balke ibadat aur Allah Ta‘ala ka zikr hai. Safar-e-Hajj o Umrah mein khareed o farokht fi nafsihi jaaiz hai, jaisa ke Qur’an mein ijaazat di gayi hai: ": "لَيْسَ عَلَيْكُمْ جُنَاحٌ أَنْ تَبْتَغُوا فَضْلًا مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ (Al-Baqarah:198) Yani zaroorat ke tehat kharidari mein koi haraj nahin, albata is mein itna mashghool hona ke ibadat aur zikr mein kami aajaye, yeh makrooh aur napasandeeda ke zumeray mein hai.

Hajj wa Umrah ke baad aap kaise rahe

Hajj wa Umrah ke baad aap gunahon se aise paak wa saaf ho jaate hain jaise aaj hi paida hue hain. Koshish kijiye ke yeh kefiyat hamesha baaqi rahe. Gunahon ke qareeb hargiz na jaayein. Haaji ki shayaan-e-shaan amal kijiye, ibaadaat aur naikiyon mein izaafa ho. Safar mein takleef aur pareshani ka zikr logon se nah karein. Fuzool kharchi, khurafaat, lahu o laib aur jhoot bolne se parhez kijiye. Rishtedaron ko unka haq dijiye, haraam se bachein, nigahon ki hifaazat karein, saadaa zindagi guzariye. Shireen kalam, naram dil aur khush akhlaaq baniye. Har roz apna muhasaba karte rahiye ke kitne kaam achche kiye aur kitne bure. Qur’an-e-Kareem ki tilaawat aur zikr-o-azkaar, masnoon duaaon aur sunnat rozo ko apna mamool banaali jiye. Dawat o Tableeg ki mehnat se apne aap ko jore rakhe. Ulama se mohabbat karein. Deeni madaaris aur deeni kaamon mein apne jaan wa maal ke zariye taawun karna apna fariza samjhe.Aurat ke liye zaroori hai ke woh shar‘i parda ikhtiyar kare, aur sirf un mardon ke samne chehra khol sakti hai jin ke samne shariat ne ijazat di hai, baqi tamam mardon se parda karna wajib hai

Hajj wa Umrah kar lene ke baad ab zyada mohtaat rahe. Agar baqya zindagi pakeeza guzar gayi to goya Allah ne aap ka Hajj wa umrah qabool kar liya.

Hajj ka aasan tareeqa

Hajj ke arkaan che hain:

1) Hajj ke Ihraam ki niyyat karna.

2) Maidan-e-Arafat mein thehrna.

3) Tawaaf-e-Ziyarat karna.

4) Safa aur Marwah ke darmiyan sa’ee karna.

5) Baalon ko saaf karna ya chhote karna.

6) Hajj ke aksar arkaan mein tarteeb.

(Tanbeeh): Zikr shuda cheezon mein se koyi cheez chhoot jaaye to dam wa fidya ke zariye talaafi nahi hogi, balki usi ka karna zaroori hai, warna Ihraam ki pabandiyo se mukammal azaadi nahi hogi.

Hajj ke waajibat paanch hain:

1) Meeqaat se Ihraam baandhna (albatta Hajj-e-Tamattu’ mein meeqaat mein nah jaate huwe Makka hi se Hajj ke Ihraam ki niyyat karna).

2) Eid ke din aur ayyaam-e-tashreeq mein jamaraat ki kankari maarna.

3) Aadhi raat ke baad Muzdalifah mein Eid ki raat ka kuch hissa basar karna.

4) 11, 12, 13 ki raato ka aksar hissah Mina mein guzarna.

5) Muharramaat-e-Ihraam, yani Ihraam ki haalat mein jin cheezon ka karna haraam hai un se bachna.

(Tanbeeh): Alwida’yi tawaaf Hajj ke arkaan aur waajibat mein se nahi hai, balki mustaqil waajib hai, albatta ek qaawl ke mutabiq Hajj ke waajibat mein se hai. (Al-Mugni: 2/285, Umdatus-Saalik: 132)

(Tanbeeh): Zikr shuda waajibat mein se koyi cheez chhoot jaaye to dam wa fidya ke zariye talaafi ho jaayegi, agar koyi shakhs jaan bujh kar chhore to gunaah bhi hoga, warna gunaah nahi hoga. Fidya ka bayan ainda aaraha hai.

(Mas’ala) Agar koyi shakhs Hajj ki sunnato mein se kisi sunnat ko chhor de jaise tawaaf wa sa’ee ke waqt seedha kandha khula nah rakhe ya tawaaf ke pehle teen chakkaron mein raml (qadam ko qareeb qareeb rakhte huwe tez chalna) ko bhool jaaye wagairah, to sawab se mehroomi hogi, albatta Hajj durust hoga aur fidya bhi waajib nahi hoga.

Hajj ke Ihraam ki qismein:

(1) Ifraad

Apne meeqaat se sirf Hajj ka Ihraam bandhna aur Hajj ke aamaal mukammal hone tak Ihraam mein baaqi rehna. Hajj se faarigh hone ke baad umrah karna. Is mein dam waajib nahi hota. Ye soorat Imaam Shaafi’i (RH) ke nazdeek afzal hai.

(2) Tamattu

Apne meeqaat se sirf umrah ka Ihraam bandhna aur Makkah pohanch kar umrah poora karke Ihraam khol dena. Ab Ihraam ki saari pabandi khatam ho gayi. Phir 8 Zil-Hajjah ko Makkah se Hajj ka Ihraam bandh kar Hajj karna. Hajj-e-Tamattu mein dam waajib hota hai.

(3) Qiraan

Apne meeqaat se Hajj aur umrah dono ki ek saath niyyat karna aur sirf Hajj ke af’aal adaa karna. Umrah khud ba-khud haasil ho jaaye ga. Qiraan me bhi dam dena waajib hota hai.

Meeqaat ki do qismein hain

Meeqaat Zamaani

Wo mahine aur din jin mein Hajj ka Ihraam bandhna durust hota hai. Yeh maahe Shawwal, Zul-Qa’dah aur Zul-Hijjah ke shuru ki 10 raate aur 9 din hain. Aur umrah saal mein kisi bhi waqt adaa kiya ja sakta hai, lekin Hajj ka Ihraam bandhne wala jab tak jamaraat ki rami se faarigh nah ho umrah nahi kar sakta.

Meeqaat Makaani

Iski teen sooratein hain:

Jo Makkah mein rehte huwe Hajj ka Ihraam bandhe, uske liye meeqaat Makkah Mukarramah hi hai. Chaahe waha ka rehne wala ho ya ajnabi shakhs, ye Makkah mein kahi se bhi Ihraam bandh sakta hai. Aur agar umrah karna ho to haram se baahar kahi bhi jaana laazim hai.

Baahar se aane wale jaise Hindustan wagairah se aane wale ke liye Hajj aur umrah ki meeqaat Ya’lamlam hai, jo Jiddah se taqreeban 15 ya 20 km pehle waqiye hoti hai. Is waqt Hajj ki niyyat karna zaroori hai. Is se pehle bhi karna jaaiz hai. Albatta Ya’lamlam ko paar karne ke baad agar koyi Hajj ya umrah ki niyyat karta hai to us par dam waajib hoga.

Jo Makkah aur meeqaat ke darmiyaani ilaaqon mein baste hain, unka meeqaat unki qiyaamgaah hai, jaise Jiddah wagairah.

Hajj ke Amaal ki Tafseel

8/ Zil-Hijjah

Makkah hi se Hajj ka Ihraam baandhne ke liye gusl karke Ihraam ki do rakat namaz padhe (Gusl aur namaz ka tareeqa Umrah ke bayan mein guzar gaya) aur Hajj ki Niyyat kare.

Hajj ke Ihraam ki Niyyat

نَوَیتُ الْحَجَّ وَاَحْرَمْتُ بِهٖ لِلّٰهِ تَعَالٰی

(Mai farz Hajj ki Niyyat karta hu aur isi ka Allah Ta’ala ke liye Ihraam baandhta hu).

Agar Hajj poora karne se koyi rukawat pesh aaye to mai Ihraam khol duga. Phir Talbiyah aur Darood sharef parhen.

''لَبَّیْكَ اَللّٰھُمَّ بِحَجٍّ ، لَبَّیْكَ اَللّٰھُمَّ لَبَّیْكَ ، لَبَّیْكَ لا شَرِیْكَ لَكَ لَبَّیْكَ، اِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لاشَرِیْكَ لَكَ''

اَللّٰهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلیٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَّعَلیٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّیْتَ عَلیٰ اِبْرَاھِیْمَ وَعَلیٰ آلِ اِبْرَاھِیْمَ َ وَبَارِكْ عَلیٰ مُحَمَّد ٍوَّعَلیٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَابَارَکْتَ عَلیٰ اِبْرَاھِیْمَ وَعَلیٰ آلِ اِبْرَاھِیْمَ فِی الْعٰلَمِینَ اِنَّكَ حَمِیْدٌ مَّجِیْدٌ۔

''اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّی اَسْئَلُكَ رِضَاكَ وَالْجَنَّةَ وَ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ سَخَطِكَ وَالنَّارِ''.

(Mas’ala) Agar kisi ka aik baar Hajj ho chuka hai to wo sunnat Hajj ki Niyyat karega.

Doosre ki taraf se Hajj ki Niyyat

''نَوَیْتُ الْحَجَّ عَنْ فُلَانٍ وَاَحْرَمْتُ بِهٖ لِلّٰهِ تَعَالٰی''

(Mai fala ki taraf se farz Hajj ki Niyyat karta hoo aur isi ka Allah Ta’ala ke liye Ihraam baandhta hu Agar Hajj poora karne se koyi rukawat pesh aaye to mai Ihraam khol duga. (Fala ki jagah us shakhs ka naam le) aur is tarah us shakhs ki taraf se Talbiyah padhe:

''لَبَّیْكَ اَللّٰھُمَّ بِحَجٍّ عَنْ فُلانٍ ، لَبَّیْكَ اللّٰھُمَّ لَبَّیْكَ ، لَبَّیْكَ لا شَرِیْكَ لَكَ لَبَّیْكَ، اِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لاشَرِیْكَ لَكَ''

Ihraam ki namaz aur Niyyat se faarig hone ke baad Mina ke liye rawana ho jaaye. 8/Zil-Hijjah ki Zohar se 9/Zil-Hijjah ki Fajar tak paanch namazein Mina mein adaa karna sunnat hai. Yaha koyi khaas amal nahi hai magar ibaadaat, tilaawat, zikr o dua aur Talbiyah parhne mein mashgool rahe.

(Mas’ala) 8/Zil-Hijjah ke bajaaye agar koyi 7/Zil-Hijjah hi ko Mina rawana ho to aaj ke halaat mein koyi haraj nahi.

Haaji ke liye Sunnat Gusl

Haaji ke liye Arafah, Muzdalifah, teen Jamaraat ki kankari, aur Tawaaf o Sa’ee ke waqt gusl karna sunnat hai. Agar gusl mumkin na ho to tayammum kare. (Faizul-Ilah lil-Maalik: 1/88)

Mina wa Arafat mein Haaji ke liye Juma

8 tareekh ko Juma ka din ho to jin logon par Juma farz hai jaise jo shar’ee musaafir nahi hain, to unko subah saadiq se pehle hi Makkah se nikalna chahiye kyunke Juma ke din tulu’ fajr ke baad aisa safar jis mein Juma nah mil sake jaaiz nahi hai. Yeh hi hukm Arafah ka bhi hai ke Arafah ke roz bhi Juma ho to subah saadiq se pehle hi Mina se Arafat ki taraf nikal jaaye, taake us par Juma farz nah ho. Lekin agar tours ya Hajj committee wale Fajr ke baad hi nikalte hain to batore majboori Juma ke badle Zohar parh le. (Al-Izaah: 301, Mugnil Muhtaaj: 1/540)

9/ Zil-Hijjah (Arafah Ka Din)

Haaji par 9/Zil-Hijjah ke zawal se 10/Zil-Hijjah ki subah saadiq tak is darmiyaan Kam az kam aik lamha ke liye Arafah mein haazir hona farz hai.

Behosh aur nasha mein mast aadmi ke haazir hone ka Aitibaar nahi. (Al-Izaah: 323)

Mina wa Arafat mein Namazon ka Tareeqa

Haaji agar shar’ee musaafir ho, jaise Hindustan ya Madina wagairah se Makkah aaya ho aur use chaar din mukammal na huwe ho, aur Hajj ke aamaal mukammal karke chaar din ke andar uska Makkah se lautne ka iradaa ho, to woh Mina, Arafah, Muzdalifah aur waapsi mein qasr wa jama’ kar sakta hai. Lekin agar kisi ka ayyaam Hajj ke baad chaar4 din ya is se ziyada rehne ka irada hai, to woh shar’ee musaafir nah hone ki wajah se mazkoora maqamat mein qasr wa jama’ nahi kar sakta. (Al-Majmu: 8/88)

Maqeem ke liye Muzdalifah mein Maghrib ki Jama' taakhir

Haji agar shar'i musafir na ho to itmam (mukammal chaar rakat) parhna wajib hoga, albata Arafah ke din mashakkat ki wajah se namaz-e-Maghrib Isha ke saath Muzdalifah mein bator jam' taakhir kar sakta hai. (Shafi'i Fiqhi Seminar: 2019)

Arafah Mein Kiye Jaane Waale Aa’maal

1. Arafah ka poora waqt ba’kasrat dua, tahleel (La ilaaha illallah), takbeer, tasbeeh, tilaawat, talbiyah, istigfaar aur darood shareef parhne mein guzaare.

2. Infiraadi wa ijtima’ee ro-ro kar duayein kare.

3. Arafah ke maidan mein qible ki taraf rukh karke ibaadat karna aur bawuzu rehna afzal hai.

4. Is din roza nah rakhe.

5. Sooraj guroob hone tak Arafah mein theharna taake din wa raat ka wuqoof paaya jaaye. Agar koyi guroob se pehle hi Arafah se rawana ho jaaye aur subah saadiq tak dobara Arafah nah laute to ek dam dena sunnat hai. (Al-Izaah: 324, 305)

Muzdalifah (9 aur 10 ki Darmiyaani Raat)

Muzdalifah mein aadhi raat ke baad kuch der thehrna waajib hai, jiska waqt subah saadiq tak rehta hai. Arafat ke din jab sooraj guroob ho jaaye to Muzdalifah ki taraf rawaangi shuru kare. Agar shar’ee musaafir ho to magrib ki mukammal aur Isha ki do rakat Muzdalifah pahunch kar adaa karna sunnat hai.

Agar shar’ee musaafir nah ho to Arafah hi mein magrib parh kar nikle. Albatta aaj kal bus aur safar ki pareshani ke Aitibaar se Arafat mein magrib parhna dushwar hota hai, to muqeem shakhs bhi jama’ taakhir kar sakta hai. Muzdalifah ki raat bhi ba’kasrat dua, takbeer, tasbeeh, tilaawat, talbiyah, istigfaar aur darood shareef parhne mein guzaare.

10/Zil-Hijjah (Eid Ka Din)

Is din ke teen kaam hain:

1.Jamra-e-Aqabah (Jamaraat mein daakhil hone ke baad sirf aakhri teesre number ke Jamrah) ki kankari karna.

2. Baalo ko saaf karna ya baareek karna.

3. Tawaaf aur Sa’ee karna.

(Mas’ala) Mazkoorah aamaal mein tarteeb sunnat hai, aur in mein se koyi do2 kaam kare to Tahallul-e-Awwal haasil hoga, yani biwi se ta’alluqaat ke ilaawa doosri pabandiyon se azaad ho jaayega. Aur phir teesra amal bhi kare to Tahallul-e-Sani bhi haasil hoga, ab is par Ihraam ki koyi pabandi baaqi nahi rahegi. (Al-Fiqhul-Manhaaji: 2/153)

Hajj mein Qurbani ka Hukm

Shawaafi’ ke yaha qurbani ka ta’alluq Hajj ke aamaal se nahi hai, Eid-ul-Azha ki munaasabat se haisiyat waale shakhs par qurbani karna sunnat hai, chahe muqeem ho ya musaafir, Pas agar haji qurbani ki istita'at rakhta ho to us ke liye qurbani karna Sunnat-e-Muakkadah hai. (Mugni al-Muhtaaj: 6/123)

Dam-e-Tamattu’ ka Hukm

Hajj-e-Tamattu’ mein dam waajib hota hai, jiska Haram hi mein dena zaroori hai. Waajib hone ki wajah yeh hai ke Hajj ka Ihraam baandhne ke liye apne watan ki Meeqaat mein na jaate huwe Makkah hi se is ka Ihraam baandha jaata hai, neez pehle Umrah se faarig hone ke baad muharamaat-e-Ihraam se bhi faidah uthaaya jaata hai. (Mugni al-Muhtaaj: 2/287)

Madina se Sirf Hajj Ka Ihraam Baandhne Wale Par Dam Ka Hukm

Agar koi shakhs Madinah ki meeqat "Zul Hulaifah" se sirf Hajj ka ahram baandhe (ya‘ni Hajj-e-Ifraad karne wala ho) to us par dam wajib nahi hoga. Use chahiye ke Makkah pahunchne ke baad sunnat Tawaf-e-Qudoom kare, phir Hajj ki farz sa‘i kar le, to use dobara Hajj ke dino me Tawaf-e-Ziyarat ke baad sa‘i karne ki zarurat nahi hogi. (Bukhari: 1524)

Dam-e-Tamattu’ ka Waqt

Eid ke din Tamattu’ ka dam zabah karna afzal hai, Umrah ke baad aur Hajj ka Ihraam baandhne se pehle bhi zabah karna jaaiz hai, isi tarah Hajj ke baad kisi bhi waqt ise zabah kar sakte hain. (At-Tanbeeh: 1/70)

Dam-e-Tamattu’ Mein Se Khana

Dam-e-Tamattu’ waajib hai, is liye is mein se khana jaaiz nahi hai, balke Gareebon mein taqseem karna zaroori hai. (Rawzatut Talibeen: 3/221)

Eid Ke Din Ke Aamaal Ki Tafseel

Fajr ki namaz ke baad Muzdalifah se Mina ki taraf rawana hona, aurton aur doosre kamzor logon ke liye aadhi raat ke baad Mina ke liye rawana hona aur kankari maarna sunnat hai. (Bukhari: 1680) Unke ilaawa doosre tandurust aur jawaan hazraat Fajr ke baad rawana honge (lekin aaj ke halaat mein bheed ki wajah se woh bhi aadhi raat ke baad hi Mina rawana hoto behtar hai). Mina pahunchne ke baad sirf Jamra-e-Aqabah (Jamarat mein dakhil hone ke baad aakhri Jamrah) ko saat7 kankari maare. Kankari Muzdalifah ya is ke ilaawa kahi se bhi jama karna jaaiz hai, albatta napaak jagah se aur Jamaraat par maari huwi kankariyan lena makrooh hai. Pehli kankari maarte waqt hi Talbiyah parhna band kar de, aur har kankari ke sath ‘Allahu Akbar’ kahe. Phir is ke baad Hajj-e-Tamattu’ ke sabab waajib hone wale dam ke jaanwar ko zabah kare ya is ke liye pehle hi se kisi bank wagairah mein paise jama kare. Phir baalo ko saaf karaaye ya chhote karaaye, albatta saaf karaana afzal hai. Aur jis ke sar par baal nah ho, use sirf ustara pherna mustahab hai. (An-Nihayah: 3/302)

Muzdalifah se Mina ke bajaaye Makkah jana

Agar koyi Haaji aadhi raat ke baad Muzdalifah se Makkah aakar tawaaf o sa’ee kare aur baalon ko saaf karaaye ya chhote karaaye aur baad mein Mina jaakar Jamrah Aqabah ki rami kare to ye soorat bhi jaaiz hai. Rami, tawaaf, aur baal saaf karne ka waqt Eid ki aadhi raat se shuru hota hai. Is surat mein tawaaf shuru karte hi talbiyah ka silsila khatam ho jaayega. (Al-Izaah: 352)

Tawaaf-e- Ziyarat ka afzal aur aakhri waqt

Eid ke din zawaal se pehle hi tawaaf-e-Ziyarat karna afzal hai. Bagair uzr ke Ayyam-e-Tashreeq tak takheer karna makrooh hai, aur Ayyaam-e-Tashreeq se bhi takheer karna aur ziyada makrooh hai. Is ka waqt taadam-e-hayat rehta hai. Ayyam Tashreeq se takheer karne par dam waajib nahi hota. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 387)

Hajj-e-Ifraad mein tawaaf-e-Qudoom ke baad Sa’ee

Agar koyi shakhs apne watan ya Madinah se Hajj-e-Ifraad ka Ihraam bandh kar Makkah aaye aur tawaaf-e-Qudoom ke baad sa’ee kare to Hajj ke din tawaaf-e-Ziyaarat ke baad sa’ee ki zaroorat nahi hai. Hajj-e-Ifraad mein tawaaf-e-Qudoom ke baad sa’ee karna afzal hai. (Al-Jamal: 2/445)

Agar Haaiz wali aurat ka tawaaf-e-Ziyarat baaqi ho

Agar haaiz wali aurat ke zimme Hajj ka farz tawaaf baaqi ho aur uske ghar waapsi ka waqt aa jaaye, to wo aurat teen sooraton mein se kisi ek soorat par amal kare:

1. Waapsi ke ticket mein taakheer karne ki koshish kare.

2. Kam se kam ek din goli ya kisi jaaiz tadbeer ke zariye paaki ki koshish karke tawaaf-e-Ziyaarat adaa kare. (Kabhi goli ke bagair hi ayyaam-e-haaiz mein ek din paaki ka guzar jaata hai to us din bhi gusl karke tawaaf kar le to jaaiz hai.)

3. Agar ye bhi mumkin nah ho aur khoon musalsal jaari ho to gusl karke safayi haasil kare aur mukammal ihtiyaat ke saath tawaaf karke sa’ee se faarig ho jaaye. Lekin is aakhri soorat mein ek oont ka dam dena hoga. Agar is se aajiz ho to ek bakra aur meendha bhi de sakti hai. (Tuhfatul-Muhtaaj ma’al-Hawashi: 4/84, Shafi’I Fiqhi Seminar: 2019)

11, 12, 13/ Zhul Hijjah ke A’maal

In teen dino ko Ayyam-e-Tashreeq kehte hain. In dino Mina mein raat ka aksar hissa guzarna aur zawaal ke baad teen jamro ko kankariyan maarna waajib hai. 13/ tareekh ki raat guzarna aur kankariyan maarna us shakhs par zaroori hai jo 12/ tareekh ke guroob tak Mina mein ruka rahe, Agar ghuroob-e-aftaab se pehle rawangi shuru kar di ho,Aur rawana hone se pehle sooraj ghuroob ho jaye. to raat ka qiyam aur agle din ki rami laazim nahin, safar ki tayyari mein mashghool hona bhi rawangi ke hukm mein hai.

Har din ke kankari marne ka waqt zawaal se shuru hota hai, aur 13/ tareekh ke guroob tak rehta hai. Aur 13/ tareekh ki rami ka bhi waqt zawaal hi se shuru hota hai. Is se pehle rami karna kaafi nahi. Lihaza jiske 10, 11, ya 12/ tareekh ki rami baaqi ho to 13/ tareekh ko un teenon dino ki rami adaa kar sakta hai. Agar din mein bheed ki wajah se kankariyan na maar sake to raat mein bhi maar sakta hai. Aurton aur boodhon ke liye raat ka waqt hi ziyada munaasib hai.

Kankari Marne ka Tareeqa

Jamaraat mein daakhil hone ke baad pehle jamrah hi se har ek ko 7 kankariyan, 7 martaba mein maarna shart hai. Aise khare rahen ke Makkah baayein taraf aur Mina daayein taraf ho. Har kankari ke saath takbeer (Allah-u-Akbar) kahe. Kankari jamrah mein bana hua dairah aur ihata mein maare, na ke sutoon ko. Mard itna haath buland kare ke baghal ki safedi nazar aaye, aurat is qadaar buland nah kare. Pehle aur doosre jamrah ko kankari maarne ke baad thoda hatt kar zikr o duaa mein mashgool ho. Albatta teesre jamrah ko kankari maarne ke baad wahan duaa ke liye na ruke.

Rami mein doosre ko naib banana

Boodha, bimaar, aur bimaar ki teamardari mein mashgool rehne wala tandurust shakhs agar 13 Zul-Hijjah ke guroob tak jamaraat tak pohanchne aur rami adaa karne ki qudrat na rakhta ho, to uske liye jaaiz hai ke apne jaanib se rami adaa karne ke liye doosre ko naib bane. Magar sirf susti aur kaahili ki wajah se naib banaana durust nahi. Naib ko chahiye ke pehle wo apni teenon jamro ki kankariyan mukammal kare, phir jiski taraf se naib bana hai, uski jaanib se kankari maare. Albatta aaj ke halaat mein agar apni teenon jamron ki kankariyan mukammal karne ke fauran baad lautna dushwaar ho, to apne saath saath doosre ki taraf se bhi kankari maar sakta hai. (Shafi’I Fiqhi Seminar: 2019)

(Tanbeeh) Naib ke kankari maarne ke baad naib banaane waale ka uzr khatam ho jaaye aur rami ka waqt baaqi ho, to maari huwi kankariyon ka i’aadaa karna zaroori nahi hai. (Hashiyatul Qalyubi wa Umairah: 2/155)

11/12/13 ki raatoon ka Asal Mina ke bajaaye Muzdalifah mein guzarna

Haaji apni taaqat ke ba’qadr Asal Mina (jise aajkal Mina Qadeem kehte hain) mein qiyaam ki koshish kare. Lekin hukoomat ki faraham kardah jagah ke mutaabiq in hudood se baahar Muzdalifah wagairah ke khaimon mein qiyam karne par majboor ho to nah is par koyi gunaah hoga aur nah dam waajib hoga. (Shafi’I Fiqhi Seminar: 2019)

Uzr ke sabab Muzdalifah o Mina mein raat nah guzar sake

Agar koyi Haaji kisi uzr ki wajah se Muzdalifah ya Mina mein raat nah guzar sake to is par dam wagairah kuch waajib nahi hoga. Uzr se muraad maal ki barbadi ka dar ho, apni jaan o maal ka khatra ho, apne kisi mareez ki teamardari ki zarurat ho ya khud aisa bimaar ho ke raat guzarna is par dushwaar ho. (Nihaayatul-Muhtaaj: 3/311)

Haaji ke liye Eid-ul-Azha ki namaz

Haaji ke liye bhi Eid ki namaz sunnat hai, albatta Haaji ka Mina mein Hajj ke a’maal mein mashgool hone ki wajah se jamaat ke saath parhna sunnat nahi hai, balke tanha hi ise adaa kare. Agar koyi jamaat ke saath padhe to jaaiz hai. Iska aakhri waqt Eid ke din ke zawaal tak rehta hai. Is ke baad qaza parh sakte hain. (Al-Jamal: 2/93)

Haaji ke liye Takbiraat-e-Tashreeq

Haaji ke liye takbirat ka waqt Eid ke din Zohar ki namaz ke baad se 13 Zul-Hijjah ki Fajr tak rehta hai. Aur Haaji ke alaawa doosre hazraat ke liye Arafah ke din Fajr se 13 Zul-Hijjah ki Asr tak rehta hai. Har namaz ke baad takbir padhe, chaahe namaz farz ho ya sunnat, jamaat ke saath padhe ya tanha. (Rawzatut Talibeen: 2/80)

Fidya ka Bayan

(Hajj ya Umrah ki kami ke talaafi ka tareeqa)

Waajib amal ke chhootne ka fidya

Kisi waajib ka chhoot jaana jaise meeqaat se Ihraam nah bandhna, misaal ke tor par, Hajj-e-Tamattu’ ya Hajj-e-Qiraan karne wala, Muzdalifah ki raat ya Ayyaam-e-Tashreeq (11, 12, 13/ zhul-Hijjah) ke teeno din ki rami ya Mina mein teeno raat guzarne ko chhorna ya al-widaayi tawaaf kiye bagair Makkah se 81 km ka faasla tay karna. To in tamaam surato mein ek bakra zabah karna waajib hoga. Agar koyi is se aajiz ho to10 roza rakhe, 3 roza Hajj mein aur 7 roza ghar aakar rakhe. (Al-Izaah: 522)

(Mas’ala) Agar ek din ki rami ya Mina mein ek raat guzarna chhoot jaaye to ek mudd yani 600 grams anaaj aur do din ki rami ya do raat chhoot jaaye to do mudd yani 1200 grams anaaj Haram ke kisi miskeen ko dena zaroori hai, agarche woh miskeen kisi doosri jagah se aaya ho.

Ihraam ki pabandiyon ka fidya

Agar teen baal ya teen naakhun ek saath nikaalein, mamnooa libaas pehnein, khushboo lagayein, sar chhupayein, sar ya daarhi mein tel lagayein, sohbat ke alaawa shahwat ke saath bosa wagairah le, to ek dam dena hoga , ya 6 muhtaajon ko 7 kilo 200 gram anaaj dena hoga warna itni raqam dena laazim hai. Har muhtaaj ko 1200 gram dena hoga. Sirf ek hi muhtaaj ko poora fidya dena kaafi nahin hai, ya 3 din roze rakhna zaroori hai. Mazkoora teeno mein se jis par chahe amal kar sakta hai. (Al-Izaah: 523, Al-Hawi al-Kabir: 4/231)

(Mas’ala) Agar ek baal ya ek naakhun nikala hai to 600 grams anaaj, aur agar do nikalein to 1200 grams anaaj Haram ke kisi miskeen ko dena zaroori hai. (Jo baal khud bakhud jhad jaaye, uski wajah se fidya waajib nahi hoga). (Al-Izaah: 530)

Fidya mein anaaj ke badle paise dena

Fidya mein agar koyi anaaj lene ke liye tayyar nah ho to paise dena bhi durust hai. (Al-Fiqhul-ManHaaji: 1/230, qiyas alal fitra)

Sohbat ke sabab Hajj aur Umrah ka hukm

Sohbat ki wajah se Hajj aur Umrah faasid ho jaate hain. Lihaza faasid hone ke baad bhi unhein mukammal kare aur agle saal fauran is faasid Hajj ki qaza kare, aur Umrah ki qaza usi waqt kare, kyunke Hajj saal bhar mein ek hi ho sakta hai aur umre kayi dafa adaa kiye ja sakte hain. Aur ek oont ka dam de, agar yeh mumkin nah ho to ek gaay de, yeh bhi nah ho to 7 bakriyan, yeh bhi nah ho to oont ki qeemat ka anaaj khareed kar sadqa kare, aur agar yeh bhi mumkin nah ho to oont ki qeemat jitne mudd anaaj mil sake utne roze rakhe. (Nihaayatul-Muhtaaj: 3/340)

Fidya waajib hone ki do suratain hain

Pehli surat: Ihraam ki haalat mein mamnooa cheezon mein se agar koyi shaks sar ya badan ke kisi bhi hisse se baal nikaale, naakhun tarashe, shikaar kare ya darakht kaate to har haal mein fidya laazim hoga, chahe unke haraam hone ka ilm ho ya nah ho, bhool se kare ya jaan bujh kar.

Doosri surat: Agar woh silah huwa yani gherao kiya huwa kapra pehne, khushboo lagaye, sar, daarhi aur moochh mein tel lagaye, sohbat kare ya bosa wagairah le, to aisi surat mein fidya waajib hone ke liye zaroori hai ke use ilm ho ke in cheezon ka Ihraam ki haalat mein karna haraam hai aur iske ba’wajood woh jaan bujh kar kare. Albatta agar kisi ko mas’ala ka ilm na ho ya ilm tha magar bhool gaya, to aisi surat mein fidya waajib nahi hoga. (Ad-Durarul Bahiyyah ma‘a Al-Anwar As-Saniyyah (694)

Ziyaarat e Madina Munawwara

Hajj aur Umrah se faarig hone ke baad chahiye ke Madina ki ziyarat ke liye jaaye, Rasoolullah ﷺ ke darbaar ki haazri badi sa’adaat ki baat hai. Hazrat Abu Hurairah ﷜ se marwi hai ke Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Meri is masjid yani Masjid-e-Nabwi mein ek namaz dosri masajid ke muqaable mein ek hazar darja behtar hai siwa’e Masjid-e-Haraam ke.” (Bukhari: 1190, Muslim: 1394)

Hazrat Anas ﷜ se riwayat hai ke Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya ke: “Jo shakhs meri masjid mein chaalis namazein adaa kare aur koyi namaz fot nah ho to uske liye dozakh se bara’at likhi jaaye gi aur azaab wa nifaaq se bara’at likhi jaaye gi.” (Ahmad: 12583, Wa qala al-Haisami fi Majma’ al-Zawa’id: 5878, Rijaluhu siqat)

Masjid-e-Nabwi ke saath Huzoor ﷺ ki qabr-e-athar ki ziyarat aur aap ﷺ par salaam ka qasd bhi kare. Hazrat Abu Hurairah ﷜ se marwi hai ke Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne farmaya: “Jo shakhs bhi mujhe salaam pesh karta hai to Allah meri rooh ko lautata hai yaha tak ke main khud uske salaam ka jawab deta hu.” (Abu Dawood: 2041)

Neez Rasoolullah ﷺ ne farmaya: “Mere paas Hazrat Jibraeel Alaihis Salam aaye aur kaha ke aap ka Rab irshaad farmata hai ke Ay Muhammad! Tumhein yeh baat khush nahi karti ke aap ki ummat mein se koyi ek martaba aap par darood bheje to main us par das10 rehmatain bhejoon, aur jo ek martaba aap par salaam bheje to main us par das10 martaba salaam bhejoon.” (Nasai: 1283)

Lihaza Makka se Madina ke safar ke dauran khoob darood shareef padhe, Haram-e-Madina aur waha ke darakhton par nazar pade to darood o salaam mein khoob izafa kare aur dua kare ke Allah Ta’ala ziyarat ko nafa bakhsh aur dunya wa aakhirat ki sa’adaat ka zariya bane. Aur Madina pohanchne ke baad gusal karke paak saaf aur achche kapre pehne, ‘itr lagaye aur sadaqa karke Masjid-e-Nabwi mein sunnat tareeqa se daakhil ho jaaye.

Lihaza masjid mein pehle seedha qadam daakhil kare aur yeh dua padhe:

أَعُوْذُ بِاللهِ الْعَظِيْمِ وَبِوَجْهِهِ الْكَرِيْمِ وَسُلْطَانِهِ الْقَدِيْمِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ ". بِسْمِ اللهِ وَالصَّلاَةُ وَالسَّلاَمُ عَلىٰ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ، اَللّٰهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ، نَوَيْتُ سُنَّةَ الٔإِعْتِكَافِ.

(Abu Dawood: 466, Al-Azkar lin-Nawawi: 26)

Phir adab wa ihtiraam ke saath Riyazul-Jannah (minbar aur qabr-e-athar ke darmiyani jagah) mein jaaye, aur is mein do rakat Tahiyyatul-Masjid adaa kare. Agar waha mauqa nah mile to masjid mein jaha mauqa mile namaz parh le aur is nemat par Allah Ta’ala ka shukr adaa kare ke: “Aye Allah! Jis tarah tune mahez apne karam se yaha haazri naseeb farmayi, isi tarah apni raza aur aakhirat mein jannat naseeb farma aur Rasoolullah ﷺ ko mera sifaarishi bana aur mere baare mein aap ﷺ ki shafaat qubool farma.”

Phir qabr-e-athar ki ziyarat kare, aur aap ﷺ par isi tarah Hazrat Abu Bakr ﷜ aur Hazrat Umar ﷜ par aahista awaaz mein salaam padhe. Phir darwaze se baahar qibla rukh ho kar apne liye aur doosre musalmanon ke liye dua kare aur Allah se apne haq mein Rasoolullah ﷺ ki sifaarish ka sawal kare. (Al-Izaah: 488)

Salaam Pesh Karne Ki Jagah

Qabr-e-Athar ke paas teen jaaliyan hain, pehli aur teesri jaali khaali hai. Darmiyan ki yani doosri jaali jaha in teenon hazraat ke naam ki takhti har sooraakh par lagi hui hai, wahi par salaam arz karein. (Jaali ko nah haath lagayein aur nah bosa de).

Salaam Paish Karne Ka Tareeqa

Qabar-e-Athar ke paas qibla ki taraf peeth karke rukh qabar ki taraf karein, nigah neechi rakhein, aur dil o dimagh ko dunyawe khayalaat se paak karke aahista awaaz mein salaam karein. (Salam ke alfaaz aindah aane wale Hajj aur Umrah ki duaon mein mojood hain).

Hazrat Muhammadﷺ

2. Saari namazein Masjid-e-Nabwi mein ba-jama’at parhne ki koshish karein. (Rasool Allah ﷺ ke zamane wali masjid ki jagah par namaz zyada fazilat rakhti hai, agarche poori masjid ka sawab barabar hai).

3. Rozana Huzoor ﷺ ki Qabr-e-Athar ki ziyarat ke baad Jannatul-Baqi ki ziyarat ke liye jaayein, bilkhusoos Juma ke din.

4. Shuhadaa-e-Uhud ki ziyarat mustahab hai, Jumeraat ke din afzal hai.

5. Jaha tak mumkin ho Madina mein roze bhi rakhe aur guraba-e-Madina par sadqa wa khairaat karte rahe.

6. Madina mein musharraf aur ba-barkat jagho ki ziyarat ke liye jaayein. (Al-Izaah: 503)

2. Nah hi uske darakht ya ghaas ko ukhaare.

3. Nah Madina ki kisi cheez ko aib lagaye.

4. Building, dukandar aur waha ke rehne walon se hargiz jhagra na karein, agar un se koyi takleef pohanche to bardasht karein ke aakhir yeh hamare Nabi ﷺ ke padosi hain.

Hazrat Abdullah bin Umar ﷜ farmate hain ke maine Rasoolullah ﷺ ko kehte huwe suna: “Jo shakhs Madina ki tangi aur takleef par sabr kare, main uske liye qiyamat ke din gawah rahuga ya aap ﷺ ne yeh irshad farmaya ke main sifarshi rahuga.” (Muslim: 1377)

6. Makkah aur Madina ki mitti ya pauda bator tabarruk le kar apne saath watan nah laaye. (Al-Izaah: 512)

Madina Se Waapsi

Madina Munawwara se jab lautne ka iraada ho to Masjid-e-Nabwi mein do rakat adaa karke dua karein, phir Qabr-e-At’har par pohanch kar darood o salaam pesh karein aur dua karke laut aayein. Dua Aage maujood hai. (Al-Izaah: 506)

Makrooh waqt (Fajr, Istewa aur Asr) mein Riyaz-ul-Jannah mein dakhla

Haji agar makrooh waqt jaise Fajr aur Asr ke baad Riyaz-ul-Jannah mein dakhil ho, to use chahiye ke woh makrooh waqt mein khaas namaz ki niyyat se Riyaz-ul-Jannah mei daakhil na ho, balki sirf haazri ya aam ibadat ka qasd kare. Phir agar wahan pahunch kar namaz parhna chahe to parh sakta hai ,usay sabab-e-mutaqaddim wali namaz jaise Tahiyyat-ul-Masjid, Tahiyyat-ul-Wudhu, Sajda-e-Tilawat wa Sajda-e-Shukr, Namaz-e-Janazah aur Qaza-e-Umri waghera parhne ki ijaazat hai, albata sabab-e-mutaakhkhir wali namaz jaise Istikhara ya Ihram ki namaz ya nafl-e-mutlaq parhne ki ijaazat nahi hai. (Nihayah al-Muhtaj: 1/384)

Bachcho ke Hajj wa Umrah ka Bayaan

Bachche par Hajj wa Umrah farz nahi hai, haa magar koyi bachchah Hajj wa Umrah kare to uska Hajj wa Umrah sahi wa durust hai, chaahe woh aik din ka ho ya muraheq (qareebul-buloog) ho. Baalig hone se pehle kiya huwa Hajj wa Umrah farz shumaar nahi hoga, balki nafl hoga. Isliye, baalig hone ke baad agar bachcha sahib-e-haysiyat ho jaaye to dobara Hajj wa Umrah karna farz hoga.

Agar bachcha mumayyiz hai (yani jis ke taqriban 7 saal poore ho gaye ho), to uska hukm tamaam cheezon mein baalig shakhs ki tarah hoga. Lihaza, jin cheezon ka baalig shakhs ke haq mein karna sunnat ya farz hai, wohi mumayyiz bachche ke liye bhi rahega. Aur jo cheezain baalig ke liye mamnoo hain, aur jinke karne se fidya zaroori hota hai, wohi mumayyiz bachche ke liye bhi laazim hain. Albatta, agar bachcha gair-mumayyiz hai (yani be-shaoor jiska taqriban 7 saal poore nah huwa ho), to uske ahkaam mein kuch farq hai. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 546)

Gair Mumayyiz Bachche ke Umrah ka Tareeqa

Gair mumayyiz bachcha Ihraam ke liye sab se pehle gusl karega, albatta iski taraf se Niyyat wali (bachcha ka zimmedar) karega.

Gusl ki Niyyat

“Main bachche ko Ihraam ka sunnat gusl karaata hu.” Gusl ke baad Ihraam ki do rakat bachche ki taraf se wali parhega.

Namaz ki Niyyat

Sunnat namaz parhta hu bachche ki taraf se Ihraam ki do rakat Allah ke waste.

Umrah ki Niyyat

“Main bachche ko Allah ke liye umrah ke Ihraam mein daakhil karaane ki Niyyat karta hu.” (Agar umrah poora karne mein koyi rukaawat pesh aaye to iska Ihraam khol doonga), phir talbiyah padhe.

Tawaaf ka Tareeqa

Tawaaf ke liye gair mumayyiz bachche ko bhi tawaaf ke tamaam sharaait ki riyayat karaayi jaaye gi. Lihaza sab se pehle wali bachche ko wuzu karaaye ga, aur bachche ki taraf se wuzu ki Niyyat wali karega. Hajjr-e-Aswad ki taraf rukh (seena) karke tawaaf shuru kare aur yeh Niyyat kare: “Main Allah ke liye bachche ko umrah ka tawaaf karaane ki Niyyat karta hu.” Phir bachche ko mukammal tawaaf is tarah karaaye ke bachche ki baayin jaanib Kabatullah ki taraf ho. Agar aap bachche ko utha kar tawaaf karte hai, to yaad rakhiye! Yeh tawaaf aik waqt mein dono ki taraf se adaa nahi hoga, balki ek hi ka tawaaf hoga. Is liye zaroori hai ke uthaane wala apna tawaaf kar le, phir bachche ko utha kar uski Niyyat se tawaaf karaaye, ya pehle bachche ko utha kar tawaaf karaaye, phir apna dobara kar le.

Haa, agar aapne bachche ko uthaaya nahi hai, balki uska haath pakar kar chal rahe hain ya wheelchair par betha hai aur aap wheelchair ko pakar kar chal rahe hain, to ab aik waqt mein dono ka tawaaf ho jaaye ga. Yeh hi saari tafseel Safa wa Marwah ke sa’ee mein bhi hai.

Bachche ka Pampers mein Peshaab Karna

Dauran-e-tawaaf agar bachcha pampers mein peshaab kare to uska wuzu toot jaaye ga aur use dobara wuzu karaana zaroori hoga. Agar dobara wuzu karaake tawaaf nah kiya gaya to bachche ka kiya huwa tawaaf sahi to ho jaaye ga, albatta ek bakre ka dam waajib hoga. (Al-Binayah Sharhul-Hidayah: 4/356)

Pampers Mein Peshaab Kiye Huwe Bachche Ko Utha Kar Tawaaf Karna

Agar bachche ne pampers mein peshaab ya paakhana kiya ho aur wali use utha kar apna zaati tawaaf kar raha ho aur woh peshaab baahar nikal kar wali ke badaan se nah lag raha ho, to Shawaafi’ ke asal maslak ke mutabiq wali ka tawaaf sahi nahi hoga. Albatta, mashaqqat ki bina par wali ka kiya huwa yeh tawaaf durust qaraar diya jaaye ga. (Al-Hawiyul-Kabir:2/265, Majmoo’ Fataawa Ibne Usemeen: 22/367)

Tawaaf ke Baad ki Namaz

Tawaaf ke baad ki do rakat bachche ki taraf se wali parhega.

Tawaaf ki Namaz ki Niyyat

Sunnat namaz parhta hu bachche ki taraf se tawaaf ki do rakat Allah ke waste.

Namaz se faarig hone ke baad bachche ko Safa wa Marwah ke darmiyaan sa’ee karaayi jaaye, aur phir iske baad baalo ko saaf karaaye ya chhote karaaye, to iska umrah mukammal ho jaaye ga.

Bachche par Ihraam ki Pabandiyan

Gair mumayyiz bachche par haalat-e-Ihraam mein koyi cheez mamnoo nahi hai. Lihaza, use Ihraam ke kapro ke bajaaye kurta paijama ya koyi doosre kapre, joote, aur peshaab paikhana se bachne ke liye pampers ke zariye packing ki jaa sakti hai. Agar woh Ihraam ki haalat mein mamnoo cheezon ko karta hai to iski wajah se us par koyi fidya nahi hoga. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 549)

Bachche par Al-Wadaa’i Tawaaf

Nabaaligh bacha par al-widaai tawaaf zaroori nahin hai. (Mughni al-Muhtaj: 2/285")

Paagal Aadmi ke Hajj wa Umrah ka Hukm

Paagal shakhs agarche bari umr ko pohnch jaaye, iska hukm tamaam cheezon mein gair mumayyiz bachche ki tarah hai. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 550)

Bachche ka doosre ki jaanib se naib banna

Mumayyiz (ba-shu’oor bachche) ka baalig shakhs ki taraf se sunnat Hajj ya Umrah mein naib banna jaaiz hai. (Hashiyatul Jamal: 4/30)

Gair Mumayyiz Bachche ke Hajj ka Tareeqa

Gair mumayyiz bachche ke Umrah mein jo tafseel guzri, wahi tafseel Hajj mein bhi malhooz rakhi jaaye gi, jaise gusl, namaz, tawaaf, aur sa’ee wagairah.

Hajj ki Niyyat

“Main bachche ko Allah ke liye Hajj ke Ihraam mein daakhil karane ki Niyyat karta hu.” (Agar Hajj ke poora karne se koyi rukawat pesh aaye to iska Ihraam khol doonga.) Phir talbiyah wagairah padhe.

Wali bachche ko apne saath Mina, Arafat, Muzdalifah, aur Ayyaam-e-Tashreeq ki raato mein haazir karega, aur agar bachcha khud jamraat ki kankari maar sakta hai to maare, warna uski taraf se wali hi maare ga. Albatta, wali ke liye mustahab hai ke pehle bachche ke haath mein kankariyan rakhe, phir uske haath se lekar maare. (Hashiyatul Jamal: 4/11)

Gair Mumayyiz ka Hajj ke Waajibat ko Chhorna

Agar gair mumayyiz bachcha Muzdalifah wa Mina mein raat guzaarna, kankari maarna chhor de ya al-wida’yi tawaaf chhor de, to iske sabab baalig shakhs ke maanind fidya hoga. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 549)

Wuqoof-e-Arafat se Pehle Bachcha Baalig Ho Jaaye

Agar bachcha wuqoof-e-Arafat se pehle ya haalat-e-waqoof mein baalig ho jaaye, to aisi surat mein uska yeh Hajj farz ki adaaiygi ke liye kaafi ho jaayega. (Hashiyatul Izaah: 550)

Hajj o Umrah se muta’alliq duaein

(1) Ghar se niklte waqt yeh dua padhe

بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَلْتُ عَلَی اللهِ ،لَا حَوْلَ وَلَاقُوَّةَ اِلَّا بِاللهِ (ترمذي: 3426)

اَللّٰھُمَّ اِنِّی أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ اَنْ اَضِلَّ اَوْ اُضَلَّ اَوْ اَزِلَّ اَوْ اُزَلَّ اَوْ اَظْلِمَ اَوْ اُظْلَمَ اَوْ اَجْھَلَ اَوْ یُجْھَلَ عَلَیَّ۔ (أبوداود: 5094)

(2)Sawaari par jab sawaar ho to yeh padhe

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِی سَخَّرَلَنَاھٰذَا وَمَاکُنَّالَهٗ مُقْرِنِیْنَ وَإِنَّا اِلٰی رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُوْنَ ، اللّٰهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ فِي سَفَرِنَا هَذَا، اَلْبِرَّ وَالتَّقْوَى، وَمِنَ الْعَمَلِ مَا تَرْضَى، اللّٰهُمَّ هَوِّنْ عَلَيْنَا سَفَرَنَا هَذَا، وَاطْوِ عَنَّا بُعْدَهُ، اللّٰهُمَّ أَنْتَ الصَّاحِبُ فِي السَّفَرِ، وَالْخَلِيفَةُ فِي الْأَهْلِ، اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ ، وَكَآبَةِ الْمَنْظَرِ، وَسُوءِ الْمُنْقَلَب ِ فِی الْمَالِ وَالْأَهْلِ۔ (مسلم:1342)

Phir **اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ** aur ** اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ** teen teen dafa padhe, phir yeh dua padhe:

سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّی ظَلَمْتُ نَفْسِی فَاغْفِرْلِی فَاِنَّهٗ لَا یَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوْبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ. (أبو داود:2602)

(3) Jab Haram-e-Makkah pohanche to yeh padhe

اَللّٰھُمَّ ھٰذَا حَرَمُكَ وَأَمَنُكَ، فَحَرِّمْنِی عَلَی النَّارِ وَأَمِّنِّی مِنْ عَذَابِكَ یَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ، وَاجْعَلْنِی مِنْ أَوْلِیَائِكَ وَأَھْلِ طَاعَتِك. ( الأذكار للنووي: 136)

(4) Jab Kaaba par nazar pade to yeh padhe

اللّٰھُمَّ زِدْ ھٰذَا الْبَیْتَ تَشْرِیْفاً وَتَعْظِیْماً وَتَکْرِیْماً وَمَھَابَةً وَزِدْمَنْ شَرَّفَهٗ وَعَظَّمَهٗ مِمَّنْ حَجَّهٗ اَوِاعْتَمَرَہٗ تَشْرِیْفاوَّتَکْرِیْماً وَّتَعْظِیْماً وَّبِرًّا، اَللّٰھُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلامُ، فَحَیِّنَا رَبَّنَا بِالسَّلامِ۔ (الشافعي في الأم:3/422)

(5) Jab tawaaf shuru kare to yeh padhe

بِسْمِ اللهِ وَاللهُ اَکْبَرُ، اللّٰھُمَّ إِیمَاناً مبِكَ وَ تَصْدِیقاً مبِکِتَابِكَ وَوَفَاءًا مبِعَھْدِكَ وَاتِّبَاعاً لِّسُنَّةِ نَبِیِّكَ مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّی اللهُ عَلَیْہِ وَسَلَّمَ ۔( الأذكار للنووي: 136)

(6) Jab Kaaba ke darwaze ke samne pohanche

اَللّٰھُمَّ إِنَّ الْبَیْتَ بَیْتكَ، وَالْحَرَمَ حَرَمُكَ، وَالْأَمْنَ أَمْنُكَ، وَهٰذَا مَقَامُ الْعَائِذِ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ( حاشية الإيضاح:208)

Ay Allah! Beshak ye ghar tera ghar hai, aur Haram tera Haram hai, aur salaamati teri salaamati hai, aur yeh jahannam se teri panaah maangne waale ki jagah hai.

(7)Mizab-e-Rehmat ke paas ye pa

اَللّٰھُمَّ أَظِلَّنِی فِی ظِلِّكَ یَوْمَ لَا ظِلَّ إِلَّا ظِلُّكَ وَأَسْقِنِی بِكَأْسِ نَبِیِّكَ مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّی اللهُ عَلَیْہِ وَسَلَّمَ شَرَاباً ھَنِیْئاً لَّاأَظْمَأُ بَعْدَہُ یا ذَا لْجَلالِ وَ الْإِکْرَامِ۔ (حاشية الإيضاح:209)

Ae Allah! Mujhe apne saaye mein us din jagah ata farma, jis din tere saaye ke siwa koyi saaya nah hoga, aur apne Nabi Muhammad ﷺ ke hauz-e-kausar se aisi khushgawar saerabi ataa farma ke is ke baad kabhi pyaas nah lage, ay buzurgi aur bakhshish waale!

(8)Rukn-e-Yamani aur Hajjr-e-Aswad ke darmiyan

رَبَّنَا اٰتِنَا فِی الدُّنْیَا حَسَنَةً وَّفِی الْاٰخِرَةِحَسَنَةً وَّ قِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ.(احمد:15399)

(9)Hajj ka tawaaf ho to pehle teen chakkar mein ye padhe

اَللّٰھُمَّ اجْعَلْہُ حَجاًّ مَّبْرُوْراً، وَذَنْباً مَّغْفُوْراً وَسَعْیاً مَّشْکُوْراً، وَعَمَلاً مَّقْبُوْلاً، وَتِجَارَةً لَّنْ تَبُوْرَ یا عَزِیْزُ یاغَفُوْرُ. ( الأذكار للنووي: 136)

Ay Allah! Hajj ko maqbool farma, gunaah ko maaf farma, koshish ko qabil-e-qubool bana aur tijaarat ko nakaam nah bana, ay Gaalib aur Maaf karne waale!

(10)Umrah ka tawaaf ho to pehle teen chakkar mein ye padhe

اَللّٰهُمَّ اجْعَلْھَاعُمْرَةً مَّبْرُوْرَةً، وَذَنْباً مَّغْفُوْراً ،وَسَعْیاً مَّشْکُوْراً، وَعَمَلاً مَّقْبُوْلاً، وَتِجَارَةً لَّنْ تَبُوْرَ یا عَزِیْزُ یاغَفُوْرُ

Ae Allah! Umrah ko maqbool farma, gunaah ko maaf farma, koshish ko qaabil-e-qubool bana aur tijaarat ko na kaam nah bana, ay Gaalib aur Maaf karne waale!

(11)Baqiya chaar chakkaron mein ye padhe

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ، وَتَجَاوَزْ عَمَّا تَعْلَمُ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعَزُّ الْأَکْرَمُ، اللّٰهُمَّ رَبَّنَا اٰتِنَا فِی الدُّنْیَا حَسَنَةً وَّفِی الْاٰخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَّقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ ۔( الأذكار للنووي: 136)

Ae mere Rabb! Tu mujhe bakhsh de aur raham farma, aur jin gunaahon ko tu jaanta hai un par darguzar farma, tuhi sab par gaalib aur sab se ziyada raham karne wala hai. Ay hamare Parwardigaar! Hamein dunya mein har qism ki bhalayi aur aakhirat mein har qism ki bhalayi ata farma aur hamein aag ke azaab se bachcha.

(12)Tawaaf ki do rakaat ke baad ye padhe

اَللّٰهُمَّ أَنَا عَبْدُكَ وَابْنُ عَبْدِكَ أَتَیْتُكَ بِذُنُوْبٍ كَثِیرَةٍ وَأَعْمَالٍ سَیِّئَةٍ وَهٰذَا مَقَامُ الْعَائِذِ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ فَاغْفِرْ لِی إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْغَفُوْرُ الرَّحِیْمُ. ( الأذكار للنووي: 137)

Ay Allah! Main tera banda aur tere bande ka beta hoon, main tere paas bohat saare gunaah aur bure aamaal lekar haazir hua hu, aur yeh jahannam se panaah maangne waale ki jagah hai. Tu meri magfirat farma, beshak tu hi magfirat aur rahm karne wala hai.

(13)Zamzam ka paani peete waqt ye padhe

اللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّی أَسْئَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَّافِعًا وَّرِزْقًا وَّاسِعًا وَّ شِفَاءً مِّنْ کُلِّ دَاءٍ.(سنن الدارقطني:2738)

Ae Allah! Main tujh se ilm-e-naafi’, kushadaa rizq, aur har bimaari se shifa ka sawaal karta hu.

Phir ye dua padhe :

اَللّٰهُمَّ إِﻧَّﻪٗ ﺑَﻠَﻐَﻨِﻲْ أَنَّ ﺭَرَﺳُﻮْلَ اﻟﻠﻪِ ﺻَﻠَّﻰ اﻟﻠﻪُ ﻋَﻠَﻴْﻪِ ﻭَﺳَﻠَﻢَ ﻗَﺎلَ: " ﻣَﺎءُ زَﻣْﺰَمَ ﻟِﻤَﺎ ﺷُﺮِبَ ﻟَﻪٗ " اَللّٰهُمَّ وَإِﻧِّﻲْ اَﺷْﺮَﺑُﻪٗ ﻟِﺘَﻐْﻔِﺮَ لِیْ فَاغْفِرْلِی اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَشْرَبُهٗ مُسْتَشْفِیاً بِهٖ فَاشْفِنِي. (الأذكار: 142)

Tarjuma: Ay Allah! Rasoolullah ﷺ se mujhe yeh baat pahunchi hai ke Zamzam ka paani jis maqsad ke liye piya jaaye, woh poora hoga. Ay Allah! Main apni magfirat ke liye pi raha hu, to meri magfirat farma. Ay Allah! Main falaa bimaari se shifa ke liye pi raha hu, to mujhe is se shifa ata farma. (Apni jo bimari hai uska naam le).

(14)Safa aur Marwah pahari par chadhne ke baad

اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ،اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ،اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ وَ لِلّٰهِ الْحَمْدُ، اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ عَلیٰ مَاھَدَانَا وَالْحَمْدُ لِلّٰہِ عَلیٰ مَا أَوْلَانَا، لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَہٗ لاشَرِیْكَ لَهٗ، لَہُ الْمُلْكُ وَ لَہُ الْحَمْدُ یُحْیِی وَیُمِیْتُ بِیَدِہِ الْخَیرُ وَھُوَعَلَی کُلِّ شَیْئٍ قَدِیْرٌ. لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَہٗ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهٗ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهٗ، وَھَزَمَ الْاحْزَابَ وَحْدَهٗ، لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَلَانَعْبُدُ إِلَّا إِیَّاہُ، مُخْلِصِینَ لَہُ الدِّیْنَ وَلَوْ كَرِہَ الْکَافِرُوْنَ.

اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنَّكَ قُلْتَ اُدْعُوْنِی أَسْتَجِبْ لَکُمْ وَإِنَّكَ لاتُخْلِفُ الْمِیْعادَ، وَإِنِّی أَسْئَلُكَ كَما ھَدَیْتَنِی اِلَی الْإِسْلَامِ أَنْ لَّا تَنْزِعَہٗ مِنِّی حَتّیٰ تَتَوَفَّانِی وَ أَنَا مُسْلِمٌ۔(الاذكار:138)

(15)Arafah ki taraf rawana hote wa

اَللّٰهُمَّ إِلَیْكَ تَوَجَّھْتُ، وَإِلیٰ وَجْھِكَ الْكَرِیمِ أَرَدْتُّ، فَاجْعَلْ ذَنْبِی مَغْفُوْرًا، وَّحَجِّی مَبْرُوْرًا، وَارْحَمْنِی وَلاتُخَیِّبْنِی، إنَّكَ عَلٰی کُلِّ شَئٍ قَدِیرٌ۔(الاذكار:139)

Ay Allah! Main teri taraf mutawajeh hu aur teri raza ka irada kiya, to mere gunah maaf farma, mera Hajj maqbool farma, mujh par raham farma aur mujhe nakaam o naa murad nah farma, beshak tu har cheez par qaadir hai.

(16)Arafah ke din ye padhe

لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَہٗ لاشَرِیْكَ لَہٗ، لَہُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَہُ الْحَمْدُ وَھُوَ عَلٰی کُلِّ شَئٍ قَدِیْرٌ۔ (ترمذي:3585)

اَللّٰهُمَّ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ كَالَّذِي نَقُولُ وَخَيْرًا مِّمَّا نَقُولُ، اَللّٰهُمَّ لَكَ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي وَإِلَيْكَ مَآبِي وَلَكَ رَبِّ تُرَاثِي ، اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ وَوَسْوَسَةِ الصَّدْرِ وَشَتَاتِ الْأَمْرِ، اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا تَجِيءُ بِهِ الرِّيحُ ۔ (ترمذي:3520)

(17)Arafah se Muzdalifah rawana hote waqt:

اَللّٰهُمَّ كَمَا أَوْقَفْتَنَا فِیْہِ وَأَرَیْتَنَا إِیَّاہُ فَوَفِّقْنَا لِذِکْرِكَ كَمَاھَدَیْتَنَا، وَاغْفِرْلَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا كَمَا وَعَدْتَّنَا بِقَوْلِكَ وَقَوْلُكَ الْحَقُّ فَاِذَا أَفَضْتُمْ مِنْ عَرَفَاتٍ فَاذْکُرُوْا اللهَ عِنْدَ الْمَشْعَرِ الْحَرَامِ وَاذْکُرُوْہُ كَمَا ھَدَاکُمْ وَإِنْ کُنْتُمْ مِنْ قَبْلِہٖ لَمِنَ الضَّآلِّیْنَ ثُمَّ أَفِیْضُوْا مِنْ حَیْثُ أَفَاضَ النَّاسُ وَاسْتَغْفِرُوْا اللهَ إِنَّ اللهَ غَفُوْررَحِیْمٌ۔ اَللهُ اَکْبَرُ (3 مرتبہ پڑھے)لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاَللهُ اَکْبَرُ وَلِلّٰهِ الْحَمْدُ۔ (الاذكار:140)

(18)Alwida’ee Tawaaf ke baad ye padhe

اَللّٰهُمَّ الْبَیْتُ بَیْتُكَ، وَالْعَبْدُ عَبْدُكَ وَابْنُ أَمَتِكَ، حَمَلْتَنِیْ عَلٰی مَاسَخَّرْتَ لِیْ مِنْ خَلْقِكَ حَتّٰی صَیَّرْتَنِی فِیْ بِلَادِكَ وَبَلَّغْتَنِیْ بِنِعْمَتِكَ حَتّٰی أَعَنْتَنِیْ عَلیٰ قَضَاءِ مَنَاسِکِكَ فَاِنْ کُنْتَ رَضِیْتَ عَنِّیْ فَازْدَدْ عَنِّیْ رِضًا، وَاِلَّا فَمُنَّ الْآنَ قَبْلَ أَنْ تَنْأَی عَنْ بَیْتِكَ دَارِیْ وَیَبْعُدَ عَنْہُ مَزَارِیْ، هٰذَا أَوَانُ انْصِرَافِیْ اِنْ اَذِنْتَ لِی غَیْرَ مُسْتَبْدِلٍ بِكَ وَلَا بِبَیْتِكَ وَلا رَاغِبٍ عَنْكَ وَلَا عَنْ بَیتِكَ، اَللّٰهُمَّ فَأَصْحِبْنِی الْعَافِیَةَ فِیْ بَدَنِی، وَالْعِصْمَةَ فِی دِیْنِیْ وَأَحْسِنْ مُنْقَلَبِیْ، وَارْزُقْنِیَ الْعَمَلَ بِطَاعَتِكَ مَاأَبْقَیْتَنِیْ، وَاجْمَعْ لِیْ خَیْرَی الدُّنْیا وَالْاٰخِرَةِ ، اِنَّكَ قَادِرٌ عَلٰی ذٰلِكَ. (البيهقي في الكبرى:9767 ونسب البيهقي هذا الدعاء للشافعي)

Ay Allah! Yeh tera ghar hai, aur yeh tera banda hai, aur tere bandi ka beta hai. Tu ne apni nemat se mukhtalif wasaail ke zariye mujhe yaha pohanchaya aur Hajj aur Umrah poora karne ki taufeeq di. Agar tu mujh se raazi hai to apni razamandi ko aur barha de, warna tere ghar se door hone se pehle pehle tu mujh se raazi ho ja. Tere hukm se yeh meri rawangi ki ghadi hai, na tujh se inkaar karte hue aur na tere ghar se.

Ae Allah! Mujhe badaan mein ‘afiyat aur deeni azmat ata farma, mera loatna behtar bana aur jab tak zinda rahoon, teri ita’at naseeb farma. Aur mere liye dono jahan ki khair o bhalayi jama farma. Yaqeenan tu har khair par qadir hai.

اَلسَّلامُ عَلَیْكَ یَارَسُوْلَ اللهِ، اَلسَّلامُ عَلَیْكَ یانَبِیَّ اللهِ، اَلسَّلامُ عَلَیْكَ یاخِیْرَةَاللهِ، اَلسَّلامُ عَلَیْكَ یاخِیْرَةَ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِیْنَ، جَزَاكَ اللهُ یَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ عَنَّا اَفْضَلَ مَاجَزٰی نَبِیًّا وَّرَسُوْلاً عَنْ اُمَّتِهٖ ، أَشْھَدُ أَنْ لَّا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَہٗ لاشَرِیْكَ لَہٗ وَأَشْھَدُ اَنَّكَ عَبْدُہٗ وَرَسُولُہٗ مِنْ خَلْقِہٖ، وَأَشْھَدُ أَنَّكَ قَدْ بَلَّغْتَ الرِّسَالَةَ، وَأَدَّیْتَ الْأَمَانَةَ وَنَصَحْتَ الْأُمَّةَ، وَجَاھَدْتَّ فِی اللهِ حَقَّ جِھَادِہٖ۔ (الفقه المنهجي:1/429)

اللّٰهُمَّ لاتَجْعَلْہُ اٰخِرَ الْعَھْدِ مِنْ حَرَمِ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ صَلَّی اللهُ عَلَیْہِ وَسَلَّمَ وَیسِّرْلِیَ الْعَوْدَ اِلِی الْحَرَمَیْنِ سَبِیْلاً سَھْلاً وَارْزُقْنِیَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِیَةَ فِی الدُّنْیا وَالْاٰخِرَةِ، وَرُدَّنَا اِلیٰ اَھْلِنَا سَالِمِیْنَ غَانِمِینَ. (الإيضاح:462)

Ay Allah! Ise Rasoolullah ﷺ ke haram ki aakhri mulaqat na bana, aur mere liye Haramain Sharifain mein doobara aana aasan farma, aur mujhe dunya aur aakhirat mein maafi aur aafiyat ata farma, aur hamein apne ghar walon ke paas salamat aur faidah ke saath loota.) (Al-Izaah: 462)

Sooraj nikalne ke baad Maidan-e-Arafat hazir ho. Agar shara’ee musaafir hai to Zohar aur Asr ki namaz Qasr aur Jama Taqdeem ke saath padhe, warna dono namazon ko apne waqt mein mukammal adaa karein. Agar takleef aur dushwari ho to Muqeem ke liye bhi Jama Taqdeem ki gunjaish hai.

Muzdalifah (9 aur 10 ke Darmiyani Raat):

9 Zul-Hijjah ka sooraj guroob hone ke baad Arafat se Muzdalifah ki taraf rawana ho. Agar shara’ee musaafir hai to Magrib ki mukammal aur Isha ki do rakat Muzdalifah pohonch kar Jama Ta’kheer se padhe. Agar dushwari ho to Muqeem bhi Jama Ta’kheer kar sakta hai. Yeh raat Muzdalifah mein basar karein.

10/ Zul-Hijjah:

Awal waqt Fajr ki namaz adaa karke ujala phailne ke baad aur sooraj ke tuloo hone se pehle hi Mina ki taraf rawana ho. Mina pohonchte hi aakhri Jamrah ki Rami karein, phir dam-e-tamattu’ ko zabah karein, phir sar ke baal saf karei ya baareek karein. Iske baad Makkah Mukarramah jaakar Tawaaf-e-Ziyarat aur Sa’ee kare aur Mina waapas laut aayein.

11, 12, 13 Zul-Hijjah:

10 Zul-Hijjah ko Makkah se lautne ke baad 11, 12, 13 Zul-Hijjah ki raato ka aksar hissa Mina mein guzaare aur in teen dino mein teen Jamraat ko zawaal ke baad kankariya maare. 13 Zul-Hijjah ki raat guzaarna aur kankariya maarna us shakhs par zaroori hai jo 12 Zul-Hijjah ke guroob tak Mina mein ruka ho. Phir jab bhi apne watan lautna ho to Alwida’ee Tawaaf karke laut jaayein.
