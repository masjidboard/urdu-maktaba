Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Welcome to Maktaba Shafiiyya. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We highly respect your privacy. The Maktaba Shafiiyya app does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. We do not track your location, access your contacts, or require any user registration.

2. Internet Permissions

The application only uses internet connectivity to periodically check for new books or updates from our secure open-source repository (GitHub) and download them for offline reading. No personal user data is sent or received during this process.

3. Offline Functionality

Our app is primarily designed to work offline. Once the books and data are synchronized, you do not need an active internet connection to read the content.
